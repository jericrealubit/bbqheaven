## Why there's a choice

Your website runs on several online services (hosting, the online ordering database, the domain
name and more). Today I set up, pay for and look after these for you. You asked to take
ownership of the website, so this page sets out **three ways forward**. Pick the one that suits
your time, budget and how hands-on you want to be.

**Whichever option you choose, these are yours:**
- Your business name, logo, menu, prices, photos and written content
- Your customers' order data
- The domain name `bbqheaven.au`, registered to your business and ABN

## Option 1 — Do the transfer with me

You become the full owner, and I guide you through every step so nothing breaks.

### What happens
1. You create your own accounts (GitHub, Cloudflare, Google, Supabase and Hostmate) using a
   business email. I'll send a checklist.
2. We book a **handover session** (in person or on a video call, about `<<2–3 hours>>`) and work
   through the [Website Transfer Plan](./TRANSFER_PLAN.md) together.
3. I move the code, database, hosting and domain into your accounts, test online ordering, then
   switch the website over at a quiet time (while the shop is closed).
4. I walk you through the [Owner Guide](./OWNER_GUIDE.md) and the
   [Menu & Price Update Guide](./MENU_UPDATE_GUIDE.md) so you can update menus, prices and
   opening hours yourself.
5. You change all the passwords and I remove my access. We both sign the handover sign-off.

### What you do and what I do
| You | Me |
|-----|----|
| Create the accounts and turn on two-factor login | Back up everything before we start |
| Provide your ABN for the domain | Move the code, database, hosting and DNS |
| Attend the handover session | Test the site and online ordering end to end |
| Change passwords at the end | Train you on the guides, then remove my access |

### Cost
| Item | Price |

| Handover and training session | `<<$>>` |

| Help after the support period | `<<$ per hour>>`, as needed |

### Good to know
- ✅ Full control, lowest ongoing cost, you can change anything yourself.
- ⚠️ You (or someone on your team) need to be comfortable following step-by-step guides, and
  you're responsible for renewals, backups and fixing problems.
- **Best for:** owners who want full control and are happy to make simple updates themselves.

## Option 2 — Hire a developer to do the transfer with me

You become the full owner, and your own developer (or agency) takes care of the technical side
from now on.

### What happens
1. You choose a developer and tell me their name and contact details.
2. You create the accounts in **your business's name**. Your developer helps you, and you
   invite them as a member. Ownership should always stay with you, not with the developer.
3. I hold a **technical briefing** with your developer (about `<<1 hour>>`) covering how the site
   is built and the [Website Transfer Plan](./TRANSFER_PLAN.md).
4. Your developer carries out the transfer, with my help where needed. I hand over backups, give
   access only through invitations, and stay available during the switch-over.
5. Once your developer confirms everything works, I remove my access and we sign off.

### Cost
| Item | Price |
|------|-------|
| My handover: technical briefing, backups and support during the switch-over | `<<$>>` |
| Your developer's fees | Agreed between you and your developer |
| Ongoing service costs (paid by you directly) | Domain renewal about `<<$ per year>>`; other services on free plans |

### Good to know
- ✅ You own everything, and someone technical looks after it day to day.
- ✅ Your developer can add new features later.
- ⚠️ This is usually the most expensive option overall, and your developer will need time to
  learn the site.
- 🔒 **Protect yourself:** never share your own passwords with the developer; invite them
  instead. Consider a written agreement (and an NDA if you want one) that confirms you own
  everything they build or change.
- **Best for:** owners who want full ownership but don't want to touch anything technical.
